# Legal and Ethical Disclaimer (2026)

**Effective Date:** 25 March 2026  
**Applies To:** All Malaysian OSINT Community (MOC) channels, documents, discussions, events, and related activities.

## 1) Educational Scope

MOC is a professional learning community for Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT). Content is provided for:

- education,
- ethical research,
- defensive awareness,
- lawful skill development, and
- legitimate public-interest learning.

MOC as a whole is not an operational tasking body and does not direct members to conduct investigations.

## 2) No Authorisation

Nothing in MOC content, discussion, or participation authorises any person to:

- access systems or accounts without permission,
- bypass security controls,
- collect or use data unlawfully,
- harass, doxx, intimidate, or extort,
- perform unlawful surveillance or abusive targeting,
- or carry out fraud, malware abuse, or social engineering for unlawful purposes.

References to tools, techniques, and workflows are educational only and must not be treated as permission to act.

## 3) Legal and Compliance Sensitivity (Malaysia)

Members are responsible for understanding and complying with applicable law and regulatory duties in their own context, including privacy and cyber-related obligations.

This includes sensitivity to Malaysian legal frameworks such as the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 [Act 709], including the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024, together with any other applicable requirements.

Legal obligations such as reporting, notification, consent handling, and cooperation with lawful processes remain the individual member's responsibility.

## 4) No Legal Advice

MOC materials do not constitute legal, compliance, investigative, or professional advice.

If you need legal interpretation or compliance guidance, seek qualified professional advice.

## 5) Individual Responsibility and Liability

Each member is solely responsible for their own decisions, conduct, and use of information.

To the fullest extent appropriate, MOC founder, moderators, and contributors do not accept responsibility for member misuse, unlawful conduct, or consequences resulting from independent actions.

## 6) Participation and Enforcement

MOC may restrict, suspend, or remove participation where behaviour creates legal, ethical, safety, or community risk.

Participation in MOC indicates acknowledgement of this Disclaimer together with [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and related policy documents.
