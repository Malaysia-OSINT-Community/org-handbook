# Member Introduction Template

Use the following format when introducing yourself in the WhatsApp Waiting Room or main group.

## Copy-Ready Template

```text
Nickname: (What do you want people to call you, can be alias)
Role/Background: (e.g., Student, SOC Analyst, Pentester, IT, Researcher, etc.)
Years of Experience: (Years or Beginner)
OSINT Interest: (e.g., Geolocation, SOCMINT, Corporate Research, Trace Labs)
LinkedIn: (Profile link or name)

P/S: (Anything else you want to share, Delete if None)
```

## Example 1

```text
Nickname: R34P3R
Role/Background: Security Analyst
Years of Experience: 3
OSINT Interest: Geolocation and Corporate Attribution
LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/your-profile
```

## Example 2

```text
Nickname: Adam
Role/Background: Student
Years of Experience: Beginner
OSINT Interest: All
LinkedIn: Adam Mikhael

P/S: Studying for Security+
```

## Notes

- Keep it concise and professional.
- Do not post sensitive personal details.
- Share only links and identifiers you are comfortable making visible to group members.
