# Code of Ethics

## Malaysian OSINT Community (MOC)

**Effective Date:** 16 April 2026  
**Applies To:** All Malaysian OSINT Community (MOC) members, volunteers, Office Bearers, contributors, and practitioners across all MOC channels, programmes, documents, and events.

## 1) Core Philosophy: Ikut Resmi Padi

MOC adopts the philosophy of **Ikut Resmi Padi**: the more one knows, the more humble one should be.

OSINT capability is a responsibility, not a status symbol. In MOC, OSINT is a tool for service, learning, and public good; it is not for personal gain, intimidation, ego-driven display, or performative demonstrations of power or skill.

## 2) Principle 1: Legality and Accountability

MOC members and practitioners shall:

- Comply strictly with applicable Malaysian law and regulatory obligations.
- Conduct all work with sensitivity to legal requirements under the **Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) 2010 and applicable 2024/2026 amendments and guidance**.
- Conduct all work with sensitivity to obligations under the **Cybercrime Bill 2026**.
- Recognise that each individual is personally accountable for the legal consequences of their own investigative decisions and actions.

No MOC channel, document, activity, or discussion grants legal authorisation to perform unlawful access, intrusive targeting, or prohibited conduct.

## 3) Principle 2: Objectivity and Integrity

MOC members and practitioners shall:

- Pursue truth through disciplined, evidence-based OSINT tradecraft.
- Differentiate clearly between verified fact, assessed judgement, and speculation.
- Preserve auditability by recording source references, timestamps, and analytical rationale where appropriate.
- Use standardised grading approaches, including the **Admiralty Scale** (or equivalent structured framework), to assess source reliability and information validity.
- Avoid amplification of unverified claims, misinformation, and sensational narratives.

Integrity in analysis requires intellectual honesty, methodological transparency, and willingness to correct errors.

## 4) Principle 3: Respect for Privacy and Minimisation

MOC adopts an **Ethics of Minimisation**.

Members and practitioners shall:

- Seek and collect only the data necessary for a legitimate educational, research, or defensive objective.
- Minimise retention, circulation, and exposure of personal information.
- Apply redaction and need-to-know handling to reduce unnecessary harm.
- Respect the right to privacy and the dignity of individuals.

Information obtained through OSINT must never be used for doxxing, harassment, unlawful intimidation, or any form of abusive targeting.

## 5) Principle 4: Professionalism and Public Interest

MOC members and practitioners shall:

- Conduct themselves in ways that reflect positively on the OSINT profession and community.
- Maintain respectful, low-ego communication across WhatsApp, Discord, GitHub, and public channels.
- Share knowledge for legitimate public interest, capacity building, and defensive awareness.
- Prioritise constructive learning and community safety over offensive exploitation.

Professionalism includes humility, consistency, and responsibility in both technical and social conduct.

## 6) Ethical Boundaries in Practice

The following conduct is inconsistent with this Code of Ethics:

- Doxxing, harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or targeted abuse.
- Unauthorised access attempts, exploitation, malware use, or fraud.
- Collection or dissemination of data without legitimate and lawful basis.
- Publication of content that meaningfully increases harm, panic, or misinformation risk.

Where ethical risk is unclear, members must pause and seek moderator or Office Bearer guidance before proceeding.

## 8) Review and Stewardship

- Office Bearers may review and update this document to keep pace with legal developments, operational lessons, and community maturity.
- Updates should be communicated through official MOC channels.
- Members are expected to stay informed of the current version and apply it in daily practice.

## 9) Final Ethical Commitment

By participating in MOC, members affirm a commitment to lawful conduct, ethical restraint, analytical integrity, and service-oriented practice consistent with Ikut Resmi Padi.
