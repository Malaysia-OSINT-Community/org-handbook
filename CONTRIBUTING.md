# Contributing to Malaysia OSINT Community (MOC)

Thank you for helping build a high-trust OSINT learning community in Malaysia.

## Contribution Scope

Contributions should support:

- Ethical OSINT education and methodology
- Reproducible research workflows
- Community safety and moderation quality
- Curated learning resources for newcomers

## Before You Submit

1. Read [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
2. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md).
3. Ensure your content is lawful, non-invasive, and educational.
4. Remove or redact personal data and sensitive identifiers.

## What You Can Contribute

- Improvements to documentation and guides
- Better onboarding or safety practices
- Tool references with ethical usage notes
- Weekly challenge ideas (e.g., geolocation scenarios)
- Clarifications for legal/operational boundaries

## Submission Standards

All submissions should include:

- **Purpose**: what problem this contribution solves
- **Context**: who it helps (new members, moderators, analysts, researchers)
- **Verification**: source quality and reproducibility notes
- **Risk Check**: potential misuse considerations and safeguards

### Example 1 (Onboarding Regulations)

```text
Purpose: Improve onboarding clarity for new members by adding a concise WhatsApp privacy checklist.
Context: Helps newcomers complete baseline OPSEC setup before entering the main discussion group.
Verification: Aligned with existing community policy docs and tested against current WhatsApp privacy menu paths.
Risk Check: No personal data included; guidance is defensive and does not enable abuse or unlawful behavior.
```

### Example 2 (Tool-List Update)

```text
Purpose: Expand the starter tool list with a clearer category entry for username investigations.
Context: Helps new members quickly identify relevant tools and workflows during initial training.
Verification: Tool description cross-checked with official documentation and existing community references.
Risk Check: Entry is limited to lawful OSINT use-cases, includes no abuse guidance, and excludes private data targeting.
```

## Prohibited Contributions

Do not submit content that enables or requests:

- Doxxing, harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Unauthorized access, exploitation, malware use, or fraud
- Evasion techniques for law enforcement or platform controls
- Publication of private personal data

## Reporting Issues

Use the issue template in `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/community-report.md` for:

- Policy violations
- Safety concerns
- Documentation corrections
- Community process improvements

For urgent safety matters, escalate directly to moderators/founder instead of public discussion.

## Review and Moderation

Maintainers may edit, request changes, or decline submissions to protect legal compliance, community safety, and educational integrity.

Repeated violations may trigger moderation actions under the Yellow Card system defined in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
