# Terms of Reference (ToR)

## Malaysia OSINT Community (MOC)

**Effective Date:** 16 April 2026  
**Applies To:** All Malaysia OSINT Community (MOC) channels, programmes, documents, events, and related activities.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Terms of Reference sets out the organisational model, role accountabilities, operational procedures, and governance boundaries of the Malaysia OSINT Community (MOC).

MOC is a professional learning community for Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) in Malaysia. Its purpose is to strengthen lawful capability through education, ethical research, defensive awareness, and practical skill development.

MOC applies the community philosophy **Ikut Resmi Padi**: the more one knows, the more humble one should be. Members and role-holders are expected to lead with discipline, humility, and service to the community.

## 2. Core Principles

- Legality first.
- Ethics by default.
- Privacy and harm reduction.
- Reproducible evidence and source integrity.
- Low-ego, community-centred collaboration.

## 3. Definitions

### 3.1 OSINT

**Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)** means the lawful collection, validation, analysis, and interpretation of information from publicly available or properly authorised sources.

### 3.2 Office Bearers

**Office Bearers** are leadership and administrative roles that provide strategic direction, legal-compliance sensitivity, and organisational continuity for MOC.

### 3.3 Four Pillars

**Four Pillars** are MOC's volunteer-led functional engines: Operations, Research & Technical, Community Support, and Engagement.

### 3.4 Yellow Card

A **Yellow Card** is MOC's formal first-stage disciplinary warning for breaches of community standards. It includes written notice and corrective guidance, with escalation for repeated or severe misconduct.

## 4. Organisational Structure

MOC operates a **non-bureaucratic hub-and-spoke model**.

- The **hub** consists of Office Bearers who provide strategic alignment, risk judgement, and continuity.
- The **spokes** are the Four Pillars that execute day-to-day community activities through practical, volunteer-led delivery.

The model is designed to remain agile, low-ego, and mission-focused while preserving accountability.

## 5. Job Scope

### 5.1 Office Bearers

These roles provide the strategic vision, legal framework sensitivity, and administrative backbone of the organisation.

#### Founder/Chairman

- Oversees long-term sustainability and brand integrity of MOC.
- Acts as final arbiter for high-level ethical dilemmas and major legal-compliance issues.
- Ensures all community growth aligns with MOC's mission of lawful and ethical OSINT practice in Malaysia.

#### Managing Director

- Directs technical vision and integration of threat intelligence capability within the community.
- Approves the technical roadmap, including projects and public-facing structure.
- Conveys operational decision-making and acts as the Chairman's representative for non-high-level decisions.
- Escalates high-level matters for discussion with the Chairman and relevant committees, where applicable.

#### Operations Director

- Focuses on community health, administrative sustainability, and external partnership management.
- Coordinates the Four Pillars to ensure synchronised execution of sharing sessions and workshops.
- Maintains overall culture quality and "vibe check" across WhatsApp and Discord channels.

#### Secretary

- Maintains official documentation, meeting minutes, and the Org Handbook.
- Manages the member database and onboarding records with PDPA compliance sensitivity.
- Handles official correspondence with external speakers and potential partners.

#### Administrator

- Manages technical backends for WhatsApp Community, Discord server, and GitHub repository.
- Supports the Secretary in file administration and community organisation.
- Monitors growth and engagement metrics and reports trends to Directors.

### 5.2 The Four Pillars

These pillars are the functional engines of MOC, driven by volunteerism and technical practice.

#### Operations

- Vets incoming members through LinkedIn and verification form checks to establish credible identity context ("who is behind the screen").
- Enforces the Code of Conduct and administers the Yellow Card warning model for misconduct, including doxxing and harassment risk.
- Moderates high-tension discussions to preserve a low-ego, learning-first environment.

#### Research & Technical

- Vets OSINT tools for reliability before inclusion in [resources/TOOLS.md](resources/TOOLS.md).
- Designs and runs technical hunts and OSINT challenges to test practical capability.
- Develops and maintains community-led technical projects.

#### Community Support

- Guides new members through mandatory Account Sanitisation onboarding to improve privacy safety on WhatsApp.
- Provides direct support on OPSEC and general OSINT methodology.
- Responds to internal and external queries regarding MOC mission, scope, and community practice.

#### Engagement

- Manages the content calendar for WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and Discord.
- Organises and moderates Sharing Sessions, Webinars, and Workshops.
- Drafts professional promotional materials and post-event summaries to sustain member participation.

## 6. Legal and Ethical Framework

MOC is strictly a learning community for:

- education,
- ethical research,
- defensive awareness,
- lawful capability building, and
- legitimate public-interest learning.

MOC is not an operational tasking body and does not authorise investigations, intrusive targeting, unlawful surveillance, unauthorised access, or abuse.

MOC governance and operational handling are informed by Malaysian legal and compliance expectations, including:

- **Personal Data Protection Act 2010 [Act 709]** and applicable **Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024**.

All members remain **individually liable** for their own decisions, investigative actions, and legal compliance obligations.

## 7. Operational Procedures

### 7.1 Membership Verification and Entry

- New applicants complete the official membership verification form.
- Operations conducts baseline verification, including LinkedIn and profile consistency checks.
- Access is granted only after satisfactory verification and onboarding readiness.

### 7.2 Mandatory Account Sanitisation Baseline

Before active participation, each member must complete the onboarding cyber-hygiene baseline described in [guides/ONBOARDING.md](guides/ONBOARDING.md), including privacy controls for profile exposure and account security.

### 7.3 Discipline and Yellow Card Model

Discipline is enforced under the Code of Conduct using a graduated model:

- **First breach:** Yellow Card, written warning, and corrective guidance.
- **Repeated or serious breach:** temporary mute, access restriction, or temporary removal pending review.
- **Severe, harmful, or potentially unlawful breach:** immediate removal and further escalation as required.

Moderators and Office Bearers may act without prior warning where urgency, legal sensitivity, or community safety requires immediate intervention.

### 7.4 Volunteerism and Merit

- MOC roles are volunteer-driven and merit-based.
- Role continuity is based on conduct, reliability, contribution quality, and alignment to MOC values.
- Appointment, reassignment, or rotation decisions are made in good faith to preserve mission effectiveness and community safety.
